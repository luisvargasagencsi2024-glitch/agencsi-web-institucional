# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite, explicitly requested for this project.

## Users

Visitors arriving at the site's public URL. The project's specific audience is still to be defined.

## Product Purpose

Provide a unique public website with a maintainable starting point, ready to grow with real site content and workflows.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The initial delivery is intentionally blank: a small, useful landing state rather than invented product claims.
- React + Vite are required.
- The frontend must not contain business logic. Domain, application, infrastructure, and presentation remain separate.
- Site-specific content, navigation, and integrations are open decisions.

## Evidence on Hand

No approved copy, imagery, brand assets, or product claims have been supplied. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Keep product rules independent from React and browser APIs.
- Make dependencies point inward toward the domain.
- Prefer small, explicit modules over framework-coupled abstractions.
- Keep the initial experience honest about what is not yet defined.
